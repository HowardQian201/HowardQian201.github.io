About

I'm a PhD student at Rice University advised by Dr. Kaiyu Hang in the Robotics and Physical Interactions Lab. My research focuses on robot manipulation learning from internet scale videos and recommendation systems for reinforcement learning. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Rice University in 2024 with Distinction in Research and Creative Works.

Previously, I worked at Pinterest, where I contributed to the bulk ingestion efforts, pin title cleaning algorithms, and sales operations data analysis.
